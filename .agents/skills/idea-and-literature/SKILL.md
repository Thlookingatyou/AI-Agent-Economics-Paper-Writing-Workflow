# Idea And Literature

Recover the economic question and existing research first. Verify primary source/version and label abstract-only reading. Build the paper's literature matrix with mechanism, data, identification, contribution overlap and transferable method. Distinguish representation, measurement, application, prediction and causal contributions. Test data feasibility against actual exports or subscribed fields; do not assume US disclosure conventions transfer to China. Compare promising ideas by economic importance, closest overlap, obtainable data, decisive tests, skills/resources and falsification. Suggest the smallest informative pilot; uncertainty about novelty stays explicit. A user request to generate ideas does not authorize full training or manuscript integration.

Read [the relevant protocol](../../../docs/first-week.md) when carrying out this workflow. Read `profiles/researcher.md` and the active paper context for preferences; current user instructions prevail.
