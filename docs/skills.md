# Skills

Repository-local skills; read only the relevant entrypoint and linked protocol. These procedures guide reasoning; their existence does not prove a database/tool is connected or a study is correct.

| Skill | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [research-context](../.agents/skills/research-context/SKILL.md) | Recover a paper's current state, prior decisions, artifacts and task scope when resuming work, changing chats/devices, or locating previous analyses. |
| [idea-and-literature](../.agents/skills/idea-and-literature/SKILL.md) | Evaluate or generate economics/finance research ideas, verify relevant literature, and assess contribution and data feasibility before implementation. |
| [data-design-audit](../.agents/skills/data-design-audit/SKILL.md) | Inspect research exports, map database fields, audit disclosure completeness, joins, sample construction and information timing before finance/economics estimation. |
| [asset-embedding-research](../.agents/skills/asset-embedding-research/SKILL.md) | Design, implement or compare asset embeddings and holdings-derived measures, including BERT/Word2Vec methods, independent validation and point-in-time prediction. |
| [ai-trading-research](../.agents/skills/ai-trading-research/SKILL.md) | Design or audit AI-trading and market-simulation research, including information structures, strategic learning, benchmarks, price efficiency and empirical links. |
| [experiment-and-method](../.agents/skills/experiment-and-method/SKILL.md) | Implement research methods or register and run authorized experiments with method fidelity, complete outputs, reproducibility and checkpoint records. |
| [result-diagnosis](../.agents/skills/result-diagnosis/SKILL.md) | Interpret completed empirical or simulation results, diagnose unexpected signs/nulls/sample changes, and distinguish measurement, implementation, inference and economic explanations. |
| [manuscript-review](../.agents/skills/manuscript-review/SKILL.md) | Check grammar, logic, claims, terminology or structure of the researcher's research writing, or integrate reviewed material within the requested authoring scope. |
| [exhibits-and-release](../.agents/skills/exhibits-and-release/SKILL.md) | Prepare research tables/figures, compact mentor packets, exhibit provenance or a reviewed replication/release package from existing research outputs. |
| [research-teaching](../.agents/skills/research-teaching/SKILL.md) | Explain an economics, finance or computational research method at the researcher's requested depth, including how his actual implementation connects to equations and economic meaning. |
| [workspace-organization](../.agents/skills/workspace-organization/SKILL.md) | Separate working files, review packets and reader-facing outputs; maintain clear navigation while preserving source paths and reproducibility. |
