# Author-controlled manuscript review

Identify the authoritative source and requested mode: grammar, logic/claims, terminology-only, alternative version, structural review, or integration. Follow the live request; a historic pause is neither perpetual nor authorization to execute. A task-state record is context, not permission by itself.

Grammar: preserve scientific content and recognizable wording. Provide exact original/replacement text with section/paragraph/equation location. Do not silently alter samples, signs, units, significance, timing or interpretation. Scientific concerns belong in a separate logic table.

Logic: map claim → evidence artifact/run → actual inference → supported wording. Check rank versus level, association versus prediction/causality, mechanism consistency versus identification, timing, units, population, denominator, coefficient interactions and uncertainty. Flag an unsupported causal leap with evidence and an alternative, not a wholesale rewrite.

For asset embeddings, verify score universe and information set. For AI trading, distinguish a simulation result from a claim about real traders, AI exposure from algorithm use, profits from welfare, and coordination from an established mechanism.

Structure: clarify the economic question, contribution, measurement, model/design, evidence and interpretation. Let substantive results determine organization. The researcher writes empirical descriptions by default; furnish an evidence packet or outline when useful, and draft only when requested.

After authorized edits, record before/after text in review/edits.md. Check exhibits, labels, references, equations, citation support and source consistency; compile and visually verify with available tools. State if compilation/visual review was not possible. Material limitations belong once where readers need them; detailed speculative diagnostics belong in internal notes.
